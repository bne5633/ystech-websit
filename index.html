About Us

Authorized agency & distribution of electronic components since 2010.

YAO SHENG TECHNOLOGY (HONG KONG) CO., LIMITED

Founded in August 2010 with a registered capital of RMB 5,000,000, Yaosheng Technology is a professional distributor of RF & microwave components. Headquartered in Hong Kong with a sales office in Shenzhen, we are an authorized agent for well-known brands including Dalicap, Renesas, ST, NXP, SPSEMI, Murata and TDK, covering RF & microwave, protection, passive components and more.

With a senior technical team holding more than ten years of R&D and production experience, we deliver stable supply channels, professional selection support and flexible delivery - from engineering samples to volume production, from design-in to mass production.
