About Us

Specializing in electronic component distribution, connecting manufacturers with end customers and accelerating product development.

Yaosheng Technology (Hong Kong) Co., Ltd.

Headquartered in Hong Kong and serving customers worldwide, we are a professional electronic components distributor representing well-known brands including Dalicap, Renesas, ST, TI, NXP, Shunlei Electronics, Murata and TDK, covering industrial control, automotive electronics, communication equipment, consumer electronics and medical electronics.

Backed by stable supply channels, professional selection support and flexible delivery capabilities, we provide end-to-end component services from design-in to mass production.
